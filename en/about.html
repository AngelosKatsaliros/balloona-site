Hi, I'm Elisavet

The story behind Balloona.

I'm an educator, and creativity and imagination are an essential part of my life! I love giving shape to small ideas and making things that bring smiles.

That's how I started making personalised bobo balloons, and Balloona was born: from my wish to turn imagination into one-of-a-kind gifts, full of love and care.

Every creation has its own little story — and I'm so happy to become part of yours!
